OUR STORY

About Gomen be’Tena

Celebrating health, culture and meaningful conversations.

Our Philosophy

They always say "ጎመን በጤና" — a traditional expression reminding us that peace of mind and good health are among life's greatest luxuries.

We believe true wellness is not just physical. It is the harmony of mind, body, spirit and lifestyle.

Our Mission

Our mission is to bridge the gap between rich Ethiopian cultural traditions and modern health science through engaging, informative and meaningful conversations.

Our Community

This podcast is a platform for Ethiopians around the world, particularly women, to explore useful health and lifestyle topics and learn from medical and social science knowledge.
